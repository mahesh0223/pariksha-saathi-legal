Privacy Policy for Pariksha Saathi

Last updated: 15 November 2026
This policy is grounded in exactly what the app and its backend actually do — it is not a substitute for legal advice, and may be revised as the app evolves.

What this app is

Pariksha Saathi is a free exam-preparation app and website for 26 Indian government exams: SSC, banking, railways, state police, defence, teaching, state PSC and state staff-selection exams. The Android app works offline without an account; the website needs an internet connection. An optional account lets your progress sync across devices.

What we collect

If you never create an account: nothing leaves your device except your study progress (quiz attempts, lesson completions, bookmarks), tagged with a random identifier generated on your device — not your name, phone number, or any hardware identifier — so it can be recognized as belonging to the same device on a later sync, and nothing else.

If you create an account: your email address and a password (which we never store directly — only a one-way cryptographic hash of it, so we can't see or recover your actual password). Your study progress becomes associated with your account instead of just your device, so it's available if you reinstall the app or use it on another device signed into the same account.

We do not collect: your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any device identifier tied to your hardware (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.). The Android app does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs today — nothing about your use of the app is shared with ad networks or analytics companies. The website at parikshasaathi.com does not show ads yet — see "Advertising on the website" below for how ads will work once they are switched on.

How we use what we collect

  • Study progress (quiz scores, completed lessons, bookmarks) is used only to show it back to you — your streak, your average score, your mistake notebook.
  • Live mock leaderboard. If you sit a live mock, your score, number correct and rank are counted on that mock's leaderboard among Pariksha Saathi learners. Other learners see only the leaderboard name you choose (optional — leave it blank to appear as "Learner"), your score and your rank; never your email or your device's identifier. Names and ranks appear only once at least 100 learners have taken that mock. The daily test shows each learner only their own rank.
  • Your email is used to let you sign in, and to send you a password-reset link if you ask for one.
  • Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties, except as described below.

Third parties

Cloudflare hosts the website and delivers its pages. Like any web host it processes your IP address and browser details to serve pages and to protect the site from abuse; it does not receive your study data.

Fly.io hosts the server that the app and the website talk to.

SendGrid (Twilio) sends the password-reset email if you request one. It receives only your email address and the reset link at the moment you request a reset — nothing else, and only for that purpose.

Google AdSense: the website carries an AdSense site-verification tag, which loads no script and sets no cookie. We have applied to show AdSense ads; until they are switched on, no advertising script is loaded — see below.

Advertising on the website

At present neither the website nor the Android app shows ads. We plan to show ads in future, and have applied to show them on the website through Google AdSense. Today no advertising script loads and no advertising cookie is set; the only AdSense-related thing on the website is a verification tag. When ads are switched on, the "Last updated" date above will change, and this is how they will work on the website:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to the website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to the website and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com, and opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Where the law requires your consent before advertising cookies are used (for example for visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), you are asked first.
  • Ads never use what you enter in the app or on the website, such as your email address or your quiz progress.
  • The Android app has no advertising SDK today. Before an app release that includes ads, this policy will describe them.

Cookies and browser storage on the website

The website sets no cookies of its own. It keeps a few small items in your browser's local storage so the site remembers your settings: your language choice, the exams you picked, the random device identifier described above (used to sync progress), the results of mock tests you take there, and, only if you sign in, your session token and email address. It also keeps copies of pages you have opened, so they load faster and work offline. Nothing in this storage is used for advertising. Clearing the website's data in your browser removes all of it.

Your data, your control

  • Delete your account from the app (Me → Account → Delete account). This removes your email and password immediately. Your study progress isn't deleted — it reverts to being anonymous: it stays on our server linked only to your device's random identifier, the same as if you'd never made an account, so the app can keep showing it to you. To have your progress removed together with your account, ask for deletion by email instead (see the account deletion page) — we then delete both.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored only on your device. If you had an account and synced progress, that data remains associated with your account server-side until you separately delete the account as above.
  • There is currently no separate "export my data" feature; your account's summary (quiz count, average score, lesson/bookmark counts) is visible in the app itself.

Data security

Your password is never stored in plain text (only a bcrypt hash). In the app, your session token is encrypted at rest on your device (Android Keystore); on the website it is kept in your browser's local storage. All communication between the app and our server uses HTTPS.

Children's privacy

This app is aimed at exam aspirants (typically adults preparing for competitive government exams) and isn't directed at children.
