Privacy Policy for Pariksha Saathi

Last updated: 16 September 2026
This policy is grounded in exactly what the app and its backend actually do — it is not a substitute for legal advice, and may be revised as the app evolves.

What this app is

Pariksha Saathi is an exam-preparation app for SSC/IBPS/RRB aspirants. It works fully offline without an account. An optional account lets your progress sync across devices.

What we collect

If you never create an account: nothing leaves your device except your study progress (quiz attempts, lesson completions, bookmarks), tagged with a random identifier generated on your device — not your name, phone number, or any hardware identifier — so it can be recognized as belonging to the same device on a later sync, and nothing else.

If you create an account: your email address and a password (which we never store directly — only a one-way cryptographic hash of it, so we can't see or recover your actual password). Your study progress becomes associated with your account instead of just your device, so it's available if you reinstall the app or use it on another device signed into the same account.

We do not collect: your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any device identifier tied to your hardware (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.). We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind — nothing about your use of this app is shared with ad networks or analytics companies, because none are integrated into it.

How we use what we collect

  • Study progress (quiz scores, completed lessons, bookmarks) is used only to show it back to you — your streak, your average score, your mistake notebook.
  • Your email is used to let you sign in, and to send you a password-reset link if you ask for one.
  • Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties, except as described below.

Third parties

SendGrid (Twilio) sends the password-reset email if you request one. It receives only your email address and the reset link at the moment you request a reset — nothing else, and only for that purpose.

Your data, your control

  • Delete your account from the app (Account → Delete account). This removes your email and password immediately. Your study progress isn't deleted — it reverts to being anonymous, device-only data, the same as if you'd never made an account, since it's useful to you locally either way.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored only on your device. If you had an account and synced progress, that data remains associated with your account server-side until you separately delete the account as above.
  • There is currently no separate "export my data" feature; your account's summary (quiz count, average score, lesson/bookmark counts) is visible in the app itself.

Data security

Your password is never stored in plain text (only a bcrypt hash). Your session token is encrypted at rest on your device (Android Keystore). All communication between the app and our server uses HTTPS.

Children's privacy

This app is aimed at exam aspirants (typically adults preparing for competitive government exams) and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
