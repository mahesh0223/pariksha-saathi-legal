Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Pariksha Saathi accounts and the data attached to them can be deleted in either of the two ways below — you don't need the app installed to request deletion.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Pariksha Saathi and sign in
  2. Go to Account → Delete account
  3. Confirm. Your email and password are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

Email sriwastava2@gmail.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Pariksha Saathi account." We process these requests within 7 days and confirm by email once complete.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted immediately: your account's email address and password.
  • Retained, but de-identified: your study progress (quiz attempts, lesson completions, bookmarks) is not tied to your account after deletion — it reverts to anonymous, device-only data, the same as if you'd used the app without ever creating an account. We keep this because it's still useful to you locally on your device; it is no longer associated with your email or any identifying information.
